## Rules of thumb

- **Bullet formula:** action verb + what you did + how (tech) + result (a number). If you have no
  number, give the scope or scale: "for 200 students", "across 9 endpoints", "35 tests".
- **Verbs:** Built, Designed, Implemented, Automated, Reduced, Improved, Migrated, Shipped,
  Debugged, Led, Tested.
- **Tailor every application.** Reorder skills and edit 2–3 bullets so the job posting's main
  keywords appear. Only do this where it's true.
- **Name the file** `First-Last-Resume.pdf`.
- **Keep it to one page.** Nobody early-career needs two.
- **Have someone read it for 10 seconds,** then ask what they remember. That should be your best
  project and your strongest skill.
